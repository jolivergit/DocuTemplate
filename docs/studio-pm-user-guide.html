Studio PM User Guide

A practical walkthrough for everyday users

Reviewed November 23, 2026 · No technical experience required

Studio PM keeps your opportunities, proposals, active projects, billing, and document templates in one place. Start with the quick start, then use the contents to find a specific task.

About this guide: This edition covers the single-page project workflow, inline setup and supporting records, the Work in motion running log, multi-person project roles, company teams, multiple firms and Google chooser recovery. It is not a completed customer acceptance test. Real Google consent, file access and generated-document formatting still need testing with your own authorized test accounts and templates.

Quick start: your first project and document

Use this fictional example throughout the guide: Maple Street Studio Renovation, for Maple Street Design, with a $10,000 fixed design fee. These are practice instructions, not records already created in your app.

This quick start assumes you are an Owner in the selected firm. Check the workspace selector before starting. Members should work on an assigned project and ask an Owner to prepare company/contact records, or use Create firm to set up their own separate practice firm.

  1. Click Sign in with Google and choose your work account.
  2. In the sidebar, open Settings → Firm. Enter your studio's details and click Save. Open Settings → Contact, enter your own primary contact name and title, and click Save.
  3. Open Companies → New Company and save Maple Street Design. Open Contacts → New Contact and save a fictional client contact, such as Jordan Sample. Link the contact to the company if desired.
  4. Open Projects → New Project. Enter Maple Street Studio Renovation and click Create and continue. The saved project opens with setup directly on the page. You are its first Project Admin. Select teammates and their project roles, then Save team. In Companies, use Add company entry to link Maple Street Design and its contact, enter its project role, then Save companies.
  5. Under Phases, fees & timeline, optionally save a general timeline, add a phase named Schematic Design, enter a phase timeline such as 5 weeks, and add an Interior Design / Fixed / 10000 fee line. Save the phase, then Finish setup. Next choose Proposals → New Proposal → Standard Proposal, name it Maple Street Design Proposal, select that phase, and click Create proposal. You do not enter its fee again.
  6. Open the saved proposal and click Load to Doc Builder. This loads its name, total, project name, and available firm details into the field library.
  7. Have a Google Doc template ready with {{project_name}}, {{proposal_name}}, {{proposal_total}}, and {{firm_name}} typed into its body. See Use Doc Builder for a sample.
  8. In Doc Builder click Load Template. If asked, click Connect Google, complete the separate document permission screen using the same account, then click Pick a Google Docs template and select your template.
  9. Click each tag in Template Tags, then select its matching value from Content Library → Fields. Loading values does not automatically assign every tag in this screen.
  10. Click Generate, check the mapped-tag count, enter Maple Street — Proposal, and click Generate Document.
  11. Click Open in Google Docs and review the result. When you started from the proposal, wait for Document URL saved to proposal. Return to that proposal to use View Document.

If you see “Picker setup required”: Stop at step 8 and ask the studio owner to finish the Google setup. You can continue managing projects and proposals without generating documents.

What you should have: A saved project, a saved draft proposal, and—if Google is configured—a new document copied from your template. Nothing is automatically emailed to the client.

Sign in and set up your details

Sign in

  1. Open Studio PM and click Sign in with Google.
  2. Select the Google account you intend to use consistently for the app.
  3. Complete Google's sign-in steps. You should arrive at the Dashboard.

Basic sign-in requests your identity, name/profile information, and email—not access to every Google Drive file.

Document access is a separate, optional step. The Google permission allows access to files you explicitly select and files the app creates. It permits editing and deleting those authorized files; it is not read-only. Studio PM's document-generation process leaves your source template unchanged and edits a copy.

Set up your studio

  1. Check the firm and role in the sidebar, then open Settings → Firm.
  2. If you are a workspace Owner, complete the firm name, phone, email, and mailing address. Members can read these details but should ask an Owner to change them.
  3. Click Save and check for the success message.
  4. Open Settings → Contact, enter the primary contact's name and title, and click Save.

Saving these forms updates the corresponding Doc Builder field values for later documents. It does not rewrite documents you already generated.

Use Sign out near your name at the bottom of the sidebar when finished, especially on a shared computer. Save current work first.

Work with your company team

Choose the right workspace

The workspace selector near the top of the sidebar identifies the firm you are using and your role there. Your original workspace stays separate when you join or create another firm. You can own several firms and be a Member in others; being an Owner in one does not give you Owner access in another.

Choose the correct firm before opening or creating a record. When you switch, the app asks whether you want to discard unsaved page edits. Cancel to stay where you are; accept only after saving forms or generating the document you need. Saved records stay in their original firm. Switching clears working screens and Doc Builder mappings; it does not move or copy records.

Wait for any active save to finish before switching. The current selection is remembered in this browser tab, not as an account-wide preferred firm across devices. After signing in again, check the selector rather than assuming the previous firm opened.

Owner: Can manage all projects, firm details, the address book, invitations, team roles and project assignments in the selected firm.

Member: Can see and work on assigned projects only, including their proposals, invoices, financial amounts, time, expenses and notes. Unassigned projects do not appear in Dashboard totals or searches. Creating a new project assigns it to you automatically. Members cannot delete projects, proposals or invoices.

Create another firm

  1. Save or generate any work you need on the current page.
  2. Click Create firm below the workspace selector.
  3. Enter a Firm name of 1–100 characters. Surrounding spaces are removed.
  4. Read the notice about the separate workspace and unsaved edits. Check I understand that switching to the new firm will discard unsaved page edits.
  5. Click Create and open once and wait. You become its Owner, even if you were only a Member in the firm you left.
  6. Check that the selector shows the new firm and Owner. The app opens Settings → Firm; complete the firm's contact/business details and save.
  7. Open Settings → Team to name the workspace or invite coworkers. Use the selector to return to your original firm when finished.

The new firm starts without your other firm's projects, proposals, invoices, address-book companies/contacts, teammates or invitations. Its firm name initially comes from the workspace name; its other firm-profile values are empty. Your personal contact, content library, working field values and Google connection remain personal. Review those values when preparing documents for a different firm.

Cancel before submitting closes the form without creating a firm. Once the server has created a firm, closing a dialog does not delete it. A firm is not a company in the client/vendor address book.

If an error appears: Keep the same name and retry in the same dialog so the app can reuse the original creation request. Do not repeatedly start fresh creation attempts. If you see Your firm is ready, use Open firm to retry opening the existing firm, not to create another one. Check the selector before creating again if you closed the dialog after an uncertain response. Creation recovery is best-effort in the current browser tab; it is not a saved Doc Builder draft.

Invite a coworker — Owners only

  1. Select the company workspace and open Settings → Team.
  2. Use Workspace name → Save name to name it if necessary.
  3. Enter the coworker's Google email address and click Create link.
  4. Copy the invitation link and send it to that person yourself. The app does not send an email automatically.
  5. The recipient opens the link, signs in with that same verified Google account, and clicks Accept invitation.

Links expire after seven days and can be used once. Use Revoke under Pending Invitations if a link should no longer work. A new invitation for the same person replaces earlier pending links. If their account needs verification, they should sign out and sign in again through Google, then reopen the invitation.

New invitees join as Members. An Owner can change a person's role using the role selector in Settings → Team. Promote someone only if they should manage every project and the team in this firm. A workspace's label under Workspace name and its business name under Settings → Firm are separate; check both if you rename the studio.

Choose the project team — firm Owner or Project Admin

  1. Open the project in the correct workspace.
  2. Click Project details if it is collapsed, then expand Team.
  3. Under Team, enable Access for each coworker who should work on this project.
  4. Choose Project Admin, Project Manager, Designer, or more than one role for that person. Each role can have several people.
  5. Click Save team. Save or cancel other project drafts first; changing access refreshes the project screen.

A coworker who has just joined but has no assignments will see an empty project list. This is expected; joining alone does not grant access to every project. Firm Owners can see all projects without being assigned. Invite coworkers through Settings → Team before selecting them here.

Project roles and firm roles are different. A Project Admin can manage this project's team and setup, but cannot invite/remove firm members or access unrelated projects just because they are a Project Admin. Project Managers and Designers can work on their assigned projects, including phases, tasks and financial records, but cannot change the project team. Firm Owners retain their existing firm-wide management powers.

Old assignments are retained and may say Roles not configured. This does not remove access or guess a role. A firm Owner can configure them. Each new project's creator becomes its first Project Admin automatically.

Company details and personal details

Firm information belongs to the active workspace. The primary Contact under Settings remains your own document contact. Content snippets, categories, working field values and Google connections remain personal, including when you own several firms.

Members normally see address-book companies and contacts only when directly linked to their assigned projects. A Project Admin can also select the firm's existing companies/contacts from that project's setup; this does not grant permission to change the firm's address book. Firm Owners manage those records. This is different from the workspace's team list.

Remove access

A Project Admin or firm Owner can turn off a person's project Access, then Save team. Only a firm Owner can remove firm membership through Settings → Team. At least one firm Owner must remain. Once a project has a Project Admin, it must keep at least one: transfer responsibility before removing its last admin, including before removing that person from the firm. An Owner can recover an older, unconfigured project by assigning a Project Admin.

The server blocks further requests immediately; another person's open screen may take up to 15 seconds or a browser focus change to refresh. Already downloaded data cannot be recalled.

Google Drive is separate: Joining Studio PM does not share someone's Drive, and removing a member does not revoke Google file sharing they already have. Review Drive sharing separately when someone leaves the company.

Read the Dashboard

The Dashboard has three useful sections:

  • Pipeline: Counts of opportunities and projects by stage. These are not a promise of future revenue.
  • Billing: Outstanding Invoices shows sent invoices awaiting payment; Paid Invoices shows amounts marked paid. Drafts are not the same as outstanding sent invoices.
  • Recent Projects: A shortcut to recently created project records. Use Projects for searching and filtering the full list.

The billing summary follows the statuses you record. Marking an invoice Paid records your update; it does not verify a bank deposit or collect a payment.

Keep companies and contacts organized

Add or update a company

  1. Open Companies and search first to avoid duplicates.
  2. Click New Company.
  3. Enter the company name and available business details, then click Create Company.
  4. Expand the saved company's card to review its details and linked contacts.
  5. Use the pencil control to edit the company's details, then click Save Changes.

For the example, create Maple Street Design once, then reuse it on future projects.

Add or update a person

  1. Open Contacts, search for the person, and click New Contact if they do not exist.
  2. Enter the name, title, phone, email, and notes as appropriate, then click Create Contact.
  3. Use the company-link control on the contact card to select an existing company. You can also link contacts from a company card.
  4. Use the pencil control to edit the contact's details, then click Save Changes.

Linking an existing person is usually better than creating the same person again. Removing a company/contact link is different from deleting the person or company.

Current boundary: Contact records do not offer a complete personal mailing-address form. Store business mailing addresses on the company record. Do not assume changing an address-book record rewrites every existing project detail or previously generated document; check the project's Overview afterward.

Create and manage a project

Studio PM uses one project record throughout the relationship, even when the work is only a lead.

Create a record

  1. Open Projects → New Project.
  2. Enter only the Project name, then Create and continue. This creates a real saved Lead, even if you stop before finishing setup.
  3. In Project basics, enter a description, status, probability, square footage and potential fee as known, then Save details. Probability expresses confidence in winning the work; it is not proposal approval.
  4. Pick the Team and collaborating Companies directly on the page. These appear side by side on a wide screen and stack on a phone. Save each section. A company entry can link an existing company/contact or hold project-specific names and addresses; it does not create a new address-book record. Custom roles and several companies with the same role are supported.
  5. Under Phases, fees & timeline, add named stages of work, optional fee lines and optional timelines. You can add ad-hoc phases later. 5 weeks and 8–10 weeks are examples, not required dates; project and phase timelines do not calculate task due dates.
  6. Save any edits, then Finish setup. At least one Project Admin is required. You can leave companies, fees and timelines empty while information is unknown. The details close to a compact project header so Work in motion is easy to reach. Use Project details to reopen the same inline sections whenever you want to view or edit them. There is no separate view-only summary or edit mode.

Search the Projects list by name and use the status filter to narrow results. Click a project card to open its full record. Incomplete setup remains expanded when you return. Completed setup stays compact. You can update the saved information without opening the old project-edit popup.

Understand the stages

StatusMeaning
LeadA possible job you are pursuing.
ProposalWork being priced or offered.
Active ProjectApproved work in progress.
CompletedWork you have finished.
LostAn opportunity that did not proceed.

Change the status in Project details → Project basics, then Save details.

Important: Manually changing a project to Active Project is not the same as signing its proposal. Invoices still need a signed Standard proposal.

Use the project workspace

The project is one page, in this order:

  1. Project details: completed setup stays compact with project

name/status, optional general timeline and active phase count. Use the single

Project details toggle to view or edit the saved information inline.

New or unfinished projects still open the

guided setup: identity/status → team/companies → phases, fees and timelines.

  1. Work in motion: the main daily-work area, with a compact active-work

summary and Add update above the running log of tasks, reminders, meetings

and notes.

  1. Documents & billing: supporting proposals, time/expenses and invoices.

Sections start closed; open the relevant section and record in place when needed.

Project details opens and closes the same inline sections; it does not save

changes. Save or cancel edits inside each section. If you close project details

with unsaved changes there, you are asked whether to discard them. Cancelling

that warning leaves the sections and your draft open. Work in motion drafts

are separate and are not discarded by this toggle.

Team and Companies start collapsed, even while setup is open. Their headers

show saved counts and a short names/roles summary. Click either header to expand

just that section; use Collapse to close it again. Keyboard users can focus

the header and press Enter or Space. If setup is already complete, use

Project details first.

In an empty section, Assign a teammate or Add a company opens its editor.

Within Companies, Add company entry is above the entries, so you do not need

to scroll through existing companies to add another.

Each saved company entry also starts collapsed. Click its name/role header to

open just that entry; other entries can stay closed. A newly added entry opens

automatically. Collapsing an entry keeps its fields and any unsaved input intact.

Choose Project role from the dropdown. Existing custom roles remain available;

choose the custom-role option if you need a role outside the suggestions.

Use Save companies to save the entries together, as before.

Collapsing these two sections only hides their fields: it does not save, cancel

or discard edits. Reopen the section to continue typing or use its existing

Save/Cancel controls. The header shows Unsaved changes, Saving… or

Needs attention when applicable. Counts and names describe the saved record,

not a pending draft. Errors offer Open to review; a missing Project Admin

warning stays visible. Leaving or refreshing the whole page is different from

collapsing a section—save your work before doing either.

There are no project tabs. Expanding a section or using a section link does not

take you to another project screen. Proposal and invoice details/builders stay

within the project, with return/cancel/save controls. Keep one financial editor

open at a time; finish or deliberately discard its draft before switching.

Phases are the project's stages of work as well as reusable proposal scope; do

not enter a second set for proposals. Company/contact information stays with

setup rather than a separate Overview. Log entries and discussion also expand

inline, with no task-detail popup or separate Notes tab.

Time/expense and invoice work still requires Active Project or Completed

status. A signed Standard proposal is additionally required for a new invoice.

If those actions are unavailable, check prerequisites before assuming records

were deleted.

Save errors keep the current editor's input available. Save or cancel before

closing an editor, changing firm, or leaving for Doc Builder. The app protects

drafts and active saves, but unsaved input is not a cross-session autosave.

Delete and loss-of-edit confirmations remain intentional. Google authorization

and file selection can still use Google's own windows.

While a financial draft is open, finish or cancel it before changing project

setup. If another user changes the project to a non-billing stage, an open

time/expense or invoice draft stays visible with a warning; financial saves

pause. A permitted project administrator can restore the stage, or you can

cancel the draft deliberately. Changing status does not delete saved invoices.

Browser Back/Forward also checks unsaved project edits. Cancel the leave

warning to stay with your draft. While a save is pending, wait for its result:

returning from a proposal, selecting another record, or switching proposal type

is blocked so an unsuccessful save cannot close and lose the form.

Build a Standard proposal

  1. Open the project, scroll to Documents & billing, and expand Proposals & contracts.
  2. Click New Proposal → Standard Proposal.
  3. Enter the proposal name, description, and status.
  4. Select the shared project phases you want to include. Their fees and optional timelines are shown for review, not entered a second time.
  5. If a phase or fee needs changing, cancel this form and edit it on the project page, then reopen proposal preparation.
  6. Review the selection and click Create proposal. When editing an unissued proposal, use Save proposal.

For Maple Street, first create one Schematic Design phase on the project with an Interior Design line at a Fixed fee of $10,000. Phases can have any custom name; no fee is required. The same phase can contain both project tasks and proposal fee lines.

Fixed versus Hourly: Fixed amounts contribute to the proposal's fixed-fee total. An Hourly line is not a guaranteed lump sum; hours and rates are entered when billing. Do not read the displayed fixed-fee total as a forecast of all hourly work.

Click a saved proposal to see its detail view. Edit changes document-specific information and, for an unissued proposal, its selected phases. Load to Doc Builder prepares its values for a document; it does not generate or send a document by itself.

Draft/Revision preparation uses current shared phase names, timelines, and fee inputs. Sent, Signed, and other issued/closed proposal records retain their financial snapshot. Use the project-scope refresh action for an unissued document; for an issued document, create an updated draft instead. Later project changes do not rewrite issued documents or invoice amounts. A new phase does not automatically add a charge or change an existing proposal's selected scope.

For older projects, you may first see Choose the existing project scope. Review the listed proposals and their phase/fee totals, choose the intended source, and click Use selected source. This establishes shared project data without retyping. Start with an empty project is an explicit alternative; original proposal records remain intact. Different existing proposals are not silently merged. An older proposal with no mapped shared phases needs an explicit selection before refreshing or issuing.

An empty proposal selection stays empty when you refresh, send or sign it, even if someone adds priced phases to the project later. On an older unmapped proposal, changing only its name or description preserves its original phase/fee breakdown. To replace that scope, open Edit, choose shared phases (or Choose replacement scope for an intentionally empty selection), and check the confirmation that you intend to replace its legacy phases and fees. Refresh will ask for this choice rather than guessing.

Proposal statuses include Draft, Sent, Revision, Signed, and Declined. Record Sent or Revision through the edit form when appropriate. Changing a status is recordkeeping, not an email action.

Record approval and additional services

Mark an approved proposal as signed

  1. Confirm that approval has actually been obtained outside the app.
  2. Open the proposal detail view.
  3. Click Mark as Signed and read the confirmation before proceeding.
  4. Check that the proposal is Signed and, for a Standard proposal, the project has advanced to Active Project.

This records approval. It is not an electronic-signature service, and it does not ask the client to sign. Use Mark as Declined only when that accurately reflects the outcome.

For the example, mark the $10,000 Standard proposal Signed only after the fictional client's approval.

Add scope without replacing the original proposal

  1. Under Documents & billing, open Proposals & contracts → New Proposal → Additional Services.
  2. Enter a clear name, such as Maple Street — Extra Site Visit, and describe the additional work.
  3. Add the Line Items, each with a description and amount. For example, “Additional site visit” at $500.
  4. Review the total and save the proposal.
  5. Record its approval with Mark as Signed after approval is received.

Additional Services uses description-and-amount line items, not the Standard phase/consultant grid. Signed Additional Services proposals can appear in the invoice builder alongside the Standard scope. Review their amounts carefully before billing them.

For a name/description correction, leave the priced lines unchanged. After

approval or billing, use a new Additional Services proposal for changed work or

pricing rather than replacing the original lines. Earlier invoice snapshots do

not update automatically, and later cumulative billing depends on those source

lines.

For a document, use Load to Doc Builder and a suitable template. The special tag {{additional_services_table}} is intended for the additional-services line-item table. Load the correct Additional Services proposal immediately before using it.

Generate consultant contracts

Before starting: You need a Signed proposal with consultant fee lines, a suitable Google Docs contract template, and an enabled Google document connection.

  1. Open the Signed Standard proposal.
  2. Find Consultant Contracts. Only consultants represented in the proposal's eligible fee lines appear.
  3. Choose the generation action for the relevant consultant.
  4. Review Contract Data Preview.
  5. Enter a Document Name, connect Google if requested, and select a template.
  6. Click Generate Contract.
  7. Open the saved contract link from the consultant section and review the finished wording.

Consultant templates can use {{consultant_name}}, {{consultant_total}}, and {{consultant_phases_table}}. The total represents fixed fees; review hourly terms separately. Additional Services line items do not supply the same consultant grid.

Generating a contract does not sign it, email it, or establish that its wording is legally appropriate. Regeneration creates another document; keep track of which version you intend to use.

Log time and expenses

Open an Active Project or Completed project. Under Documents & billing,

expand Time & Expenses.

Add time

  1. In Hours, click Add.
  2. Enter the date, description, hours, and Rate/hr.
  3. Click Save.
  4. Check that the saved row is correct. An Unattached badge means it has not yet been assigned to an invoice.

For Maple Street, record 2 hours of extra coordination at $100/hour only if this is genuinely additional billable work.

Add an expense

  1. In Expenses, click Add.
  2. Enter the date and choose the type: Mileage, Parking, Shipping, or Printing.
  3. For Mileage, enter miles and the rate per mile. For the other types, enter the dollar amount.
  4. Click Save and check the entry.

Use the mileage rate approved by your studio; the app's default is not tax or reimbursement advice. For the example, add $20 of Parking.

Correct entries and avoid duplicate billing

Use an entry's pencil control to edit it, then click Save. Use the trash can only if the entry should truly be removed. An Invoice #… badge identifies an attached entry.

Billing caution: Editing or deleting an attached entry can change the invoice's data and totals. Review the invoice afterward. A Google Doc generated earlier is a separate copy and does not update automatically.

Do not log the same work both as an hourly proposal billing line and as additional Hours unless both charges are genuinely intended. Attach existing entries in the invoice builder instead of retyping them.

Create and manage invoices

Before you start

The project must be Active Project or Completed, and it must have a Signed Standard proposal. An Additional Services proposal alone does not unlock New Invoice. If there is more than one Signed Standard proposal, verify which scope the builder is using before entering amounts.

Build an invoice

  1. Under Documents & billing, expand Invoices and click New Invoice.
  2. Review the proposal fee rows.
  3. For Fixed fees, enter the cumulative percentage complete to date—not just this month's percentage.
  4. For Hourly fees, enter hours and rate.
  5. Review any signed Additional Services rows separately.
  6. Expand Attach Project Entries if it appears. Check the existing Unattached hours and expenses you want to include.
  7. Add any genuinely new hours or expenses in the builder, without duplicating attached entries.
  8. Review Invoice Summary, including every subtotal, the total, and any internal notes.
  9. Click Create Invoice.

Example: For the $10,000 fixed fee at 25% complete, earned fees are $2,500. With no previous billing, this invoice's fixed-fee charge is $2,500. If you attach the example's $200 Hours and $20 Parking entries, the total should be $2,720, excluding any additional services.

On a later invoice, 50% complete means $5,000 earned to date. If previous fixed-fee billing was $2,500, the new fixed-fee charge should be $2,500. Signed Additional Services also use cumulative percentages and subtract their previous billing. Always review previous/current charges rather than relying only on the percentage. Wait for billing information to load; if it cannot load, use Retry instead of assuming nothing has been billed.

Saved invoices capture billing information at creation. Do not assume later proposal edits recalculate existing invoices.

Generate the invoice document

  1. Open the saved invoice.
  2. Click Generate Doc (or Regenerate Doc if it already has a document).
  3. Connect Google if needed and pick a Google Docs invoice template.
  4. Click Generate & Save.
  5. Use View Doc to open the linked result and check all values before sharing.

This route generates inside the invoice view; it is not the proposal's Load to Doc Builder workflow. It automatically matches field names such as {{invoice_number}}, {{project_name}}, {{client_company}}, and {{invoice_grand_total}}.

Template limitation: Use a prepared invoice template with the exact supported field names. Unmatched fields and content tags are not interactively mapped in this flow and may be blank. Check line-item rows, additional services, totals, client details, and firm details in the generated document before using it.

Track sending and payment

An invoice starts as Draft. After actually sending it yourself, click Mark as Sent. After confirming receipt of payment, click Mark as Paid.

Neither action sends email or processes payment. The detail view advances statuses forward; it does not offer a general-purpose invoice editor or a reverse-status control. Ask the studio owner before correcting a sent or paid invoice.

Use the Work in motion log

Create phases once

  1. Open Project details, find Phases, fees & timeline and click Add phase.
  2. Choose Phase name from the dropdown, or select Other / custom and enter an ad-hoc name. Ad-hoc phases can be added at any point; you are not limited to the suggested list. The same choices are available when editing a phase.
  3. Optionally enter Estimated timeline, such as 5 weeks or 8–10 weeks. Leave it blank if unknown. This is an estimate, not a required start/end date or an automatic task schedule.
  4. Optionally add a fee line, choose Consultant or service from its dropdown (or Other / custom for your own name), choose Fixed or Hourly, and save. More fee rows can be added through the phase's Edit controls.
  5. Use phase controls to edit, reorder, archive, or restore phases. Archiving preserves existing task references; archived phases cannot be assigned to new tasks.

General timeline is a compact field above the phase list. Save timeline

and Cancel appear when you change it. Timelines remain optional text estimates,

not automatically calculated calendar dates.

Saved phases use compact rows. Select a phase's fee lines disclosure to see

its consultant/service breakdown. Fixed-fee totals exclude Hourly reference

amounts and appear only when all of that phase's Fixed lines are priced.

An unpriced line remains marked Unpriced. Use Edit for the phase's full

name, timeline and fee controls.

The same project phases and fees are reused when preparing proposals. Changing their timeline does not change task due dates. An Hourly line is not a guaranteed fixed fee; hours and rates are entered when billing.

Choose the right kind of entry

Click Add update in Work in motion, then choose Task, Reminder, Meeting or

Note in the inline composer. Everything is written and edited on the project

page.

KindWhat to enter
TaskA title, optional description, phase and assignee, priority, status and optional due date.
ReminderA title and optional details/due date. Mark it complete when handled; no priority is required. This is a record, not a delivered notification.
MeetingA title or meeting notes, plus an optional manually entered meeting date/time. A time needs a date. No invitation is sent or calendar event created.
NoteWrite the note itself. No task title, priority, status or due date is required.

Any kind can optionally reference a phase and an eligible teammate. Leave them

blank when they do not apply. Choosing a role with several people never chooses

one person for you. Address-book contacts are not app teammates; referencing a

person does not grant access to the project.

Review the draft, then save it. Entries appear newest-created first with their

kind and recorded time. A meeting's manually entered date/time is displayed as

entered, not converted into a calendar appointment. Older tasks appear once in

this same log, with their original IDs, due dates, assignments and discussions.

Older records with no recorded author are not attributed to an invented author.

Edit, complete and archive

Expand an entry's edit controls, make the appropriate changes and save. Tasks

keep To do, In progress, Blocked, Done and Low, Normal, High, Urgent.

Reminders use incomplete/complete; meetings and notes have no completion status.

An entry's kind is fixed after saving to avoid losing type-specific information.

Use completion controls for tasks/reminders. Archive hides an entry without

deleting its history; use the Active / Archived selector to switch views and restore it when needed. Search

the log, choose a kind or use Actionable to focus on tasks/reminders. Mine

shows entries naming you as their teammate/assignee, not every note you authored.

Counts include only non-archived tasks and reminders. Meetings and notes are

never overdue or Done. A due date before today on your device is overdue unless

the item is complete; due today is not overdue.

The compact Active work summary shows open, overdue, blocked and done counts

for the whole active project log. Searching or switching to Archived does not change the active

summary. Entries remain newest-created first, not grouped or reordered by status.

Blocked and overdue work have visible text labels; notes emphasize the note text

rather than requiring a title.

Next meeting appears only when a non-archived meeting has a date today or

later. A timed meeting earlier today is skipped; a date-only meeting remains

upcoming for that day and says Time not set, not “all day.” Dates and entered

times are interpreted in your device's local time without shifting to UTC.

Undated meetings stay in the log but do not appear as the next meeting. No

calendar invitation or reminder is sent. The summary refreshes as time passes

and when you return to the window. If a background refresh fails, the app labels

the saved entries as not freshly refreshed and keeps open drafts available;

loss of project access still blocks the log.

Discuss an entry and recover safely

Open Discussion on the relevant entry and add your update there. Discussion

is dated and attributed, not copied into another top-level log entry. It is

internal recordkeeping, not a message sent to a client. Saved discussion is

append-only; add a correction when needed.

Save or cancel before opening another editor. If closing would discard text,

the app asks inline. Active saves block conflicting actions and firm switching.

Team-access changes wait for your drafts to be saved or cancelled.

Failed saves preserve text. Retry the same entry/discussion rather than creating

another copy. If the app is unsure whether a save arrived, check the saved log

using its recovery action. If another user changed an entry, review **Reload

latest** before replacing your draft. Do not refresh the whole page to recover

unsaved text; drafts are not a cross-session autosave.

The retired standalone Project Notes feature is not restored by this change.

Its earlier approved cleanup did not authorize deleting newer task discussions.

All task-era discussions remain attached to their original entries.

Use Doc Builder

Understand templates, tags, and mappings

A template is a Google Doc with placeholders. A tag is a placeholder. A mapping tells Studio PM which saved value or text should replace that placeholder.

TagWhat it insertsExample
{{project_name}}A short field valueMaple Street Studio Renovation
{{proposal_total}}A loaded proposal value$10,000
<<introduction>>A reusable formatted content passageYour standard introduction

Keep tag names consistent, including spelling and underscores. Field tags use double curly braces; content tags use double angle brackets.

Prepare a simple template

  1. In Google Docs, create or copy a normal Google Doc that your signed-in account may read and copy.
  2. Type the example below into its document body. Keep each tag together and avoid applying different formatting to parts of the same tag.
  3. Add your desired normal text and formatting around the tags.
  4. Give the template a recognizable name, such as Studio PM — Proposal Template.
{{firm_name}}

Proposal: {{proposal_name}}
Project: {{project_name}}
Proposed fixed fee: {{proposal_total}}

<<introduction>>

Google's picker in this app selects Google Docs, not PDFs or arbitrary Word files. Have the owner prepare a suitable Google Doc template if necessary. Complex headers, footers, or layouts may need extra checking; start with tags in the document body.

Prepare reusable field values and content

  1. Open Settings → Doc Builder.
  2. In Content Library, choose Fields.
  3. Click + Field, enter the field name without braces and its value, then click Save Field. For example, use project_name and Maple Street Studio Renovation.
  4. Switch to Content, click + Content, give the passage a title, enter its formatted text, and click Save Content. An optional category helps organize it.
  5. Use search and the category filter to find saved material later. Pencil controls edit saved items; Manage Fields and Manage Categories offer additional organization.

Saving a library item makes it reusable; it does not mean it is assigned to the current template. Selecting a tag and then selecting its library item performs that assignment.

Content can itself contain field tags, such as {{firm_name}}. Review the corresponding field values before generating. Changing a saved personal library value can affect your later documents that use it, but not copies already created. The library is not automatically shared with your firm's team.

Load the right record, then the template

For a proposal document, first open that proposal and click Load to Doc Builder. This replaces relevant previously loaded proposal fields with the current record's values and preserves the return link to that proposal.

Then:

  1. Click Load Template.
  2. If you see Connect Google, click it. In the popup, choose the same account used to sign in and approve selected-file document access.
  3. Click Pick a Google Docs template and choose your template.
  4. Wait for the app to read its tags. The selected template name appears at the top.

You must select the file in Google's picker. An old saved file ID or a pasted document link by itself does not grant the app permission.

Choose a template in a separate window

Connecting authorizes selected-file document access; picking chooses a particular template. A Connected message alone does not prove the chooser can open or read a file.

  1. Keep the original Doc Builder, invoice or contract screen open.
  2. Connect Google first if requested. Under the picker control, click Choose in separate window.
  3. Allow the app's popup if your browser blocks it. In the new window, click Pick a Google Docs template and make your selection.
  4. The selection returns to the original page, the separate window closes, and the original page reads the template's tags.
  5. Use Cancel chooser, Google's Cancel, or close the separate window to cancel. The existing template and working choices should remain; loading a different template successfully is an intentional replacement.

If the separate window says it cannot use your Studio PM sign-in, open the app in its own browser tab, sign in there and retry the chooser. Do not refresh the original working page as your first recovery step.

Blank thumbnails: A checkerboard or blank preview with a filename underneath is a missing preview image, not a missing file entry. Google supplies thumbnails and some may be unavailable. Try Google's list-view control at the upper right, identify the file by name and select it. A successful selection and loaded tags confirm access more reliably than its thumbnail. Do not expand Drive permissions just to get previews.

Map and review every tag

  1. In Template Tags, click {{project_name}}.
  2. In Content Library → Fields, click the matching field value.
  3. Repeat for the other field tags, checking the actual displayed values.
  4. Click <<introduction>>, then choose your saved introduction in Content Library → Content.
  5. Use a tag's editing control if you need custom text for this document. Remove an incorrect assignment and select the correct one.
  6. Review any recognized sections and their order. Where reorder handles are available, drag carefully and check the generated result.

On smaller screens, switch between the tags and content panels with the bottom controls. Your current tag selection tells the library which kind of item to show.

Do not rely only on Generate being enabled. It becomes available once at least one tag is mapped; that does not prove every required tag is filled. Review all tags and the mapped-tag count yourself.

Generate, review, and keep the link

  1. Click Generate.
  2. Review the template name and mapped-tag count.
  3. Enter a clear Document Name. Use a project name and purpose so you can find the copy later.
  4. Click Generate Document and wait for success.
  5. Click Open in Google Docs. Check wording, dates, amounts, blank fields, tables, and formatting.
  6. If you started from a proposal, wait for Document URL saved to proposal. Later, open that proposal and click View Document.
  7. If you opened Doc Builder directly from Settings, keep the generated Google Docs link yourself; that standalone route does not automatically link it to a project or proposal.

Generation creates a new copy. Repeating it can create multiple documents; an older copy is not automatically replaced. The proposal or invoice link may point to the latest successfully saved copy.

Special tables

Prepared templates may use {{proposal_phases_table}}, {{additional_services_table}}, or {{consultant_phases_table}} for their corresponding workflows. Have the studio owner prepare and check these templates. Do not type internal table data into the field library by hand or assume an ordinary short-text field can build a correct table.

Save safely and avoid accidental changes

  • Forms: Use the visible Save, Save Changes, Create, or Post action and wait for success before leaving.
  • Immediate changes: Project status buttons, invoice status buttons, and some delete controls take effect when clicked. Read confirmation prompts where present.
  • Doc Builder working choices: Template selection, mappings, custom text, and section order are working-page choices, not a saved document draft. Navigating away, refreshing, or loading another template can clear them. Generate your document before leaving and save reusable text in the library.
  • Reconnect cancellation: Closing Google consent or cancelling the picker should keep the current working page and existing choices. This is different from refreshing the whole page.
  • Firm switching: Save or generate first. Manual switching asks for confirmation, and Create firm requires acknowledgement; neither creates a recoverable document draft. A failed or cancelled Google chooser is not a reason to switch firms.
  • Existing Google Docs: Editing an app record does not update a previously generated Google Doc. Review whether you need a new document and avoid sending an outdated version.
  • Delete: There is no general user-facing trash/restore feature. Deleting linked companies, contacts, projects, proposals, or invoices may affect related information. Ask the owner before removing financial records or records still used elsewhere.
  • Privacy: Do not paste passwords, payment-card details, or other secrets into notes, content snippets, or document fields.

Troubleshooting

I cannot find my project

Check the selected firm and Google account first. Then open Projects, clear the search, and reset the status filter. Check Completed and Lost as well as Active Project. Members should ask an Owner to check their assignment; an unassigned project is intentionally hidden.

I cannot find Team settings or assignment controls

Check both your firm role and your project roles. Settings → Team is for firm Owners. The project's inline Team is editable by a firm Owner or that project's Project Admin. Owners can hold project roles too, but keep firm-wide access even if not assigned. Invite coworkers into the firm before selecting them for a project.

I created a firm but cannot open it

If the creation dialog says the firm exists, use Open firm to retry switching. If a response was lost, retry the same name in the same dialog and check the selector before starting over. Cancel before submitting creates nothing, but closing the dialog after the server created a firm does not remove it.

Time & Expenses or Invoices is missing

These sections appear only for Active Project and Completed records. Check Project details → Project basics. Do not change a status merely to make a section appear unless it accurately describes the work.

There is no New Invoice button

Check for a Signed Standard proposal on the same project. Manually setting the project to Active Project or signing only Additional Services is insufficient.

I see “Picker setup required”

This is an owner configuration issue, not a problem with your template. Ask the owner to finish the Google setup described in the separate setup guide. Do not approve account-wide Drive permissions as a workaround.

The Google popup does not open, closes, or says the account is wrong

Allow popups for Studio PM in your browser, then try Connect Google or Reconnect Google again. Choose the same Google account you used for Studio PM. If the request expired, start a new connection attempt from the app.

Cancelling the popup should not discard current mappings. Avoid refreshing the whole page just to retry.

If you are already connected but the template chooser fails, try Choose in separate window instead. Use Cancel chooser if an opening attempt is stuck. If Google reports an API configuration error, ask the app operator to check its setup; do not approve broader access or remove browser security restrictions as a workaround.

Some Google document previews are blank

If filenames and document icons are present, those entries are available in the chooser even though Google has not displayed their thumbnails. Switch to list view or select the known filename. If selection cannot read its tags, follow the file-access checks below; the screenshot alone does not identify the reason for a blank preview.

The app mentions an older broad Drive grant

  1. Save your app work before interrupting the connection.
  2. Open Google Account third-party connections.
  3. Find this app and remove its access. This revokes the old connection and may affect sessions or other apps sharing that Google login client.
  4. Return to Studio PM, sign in if needed, reconnect Google, and select the template again.

Removing the grant does not delete your studio records or existing Google documents. Simply changing the app's requested permissions does not remove an old grant automatically.

My template is missing or access is denied

Check that it is a Google Doc, that the signed-in Google account can open and copy it, and that sharing/copy restrictions allow the operation. Reconnect if requested and select the same file again in the picker. Studio PM cannot bypass Google file permissions.

If generation failed, use the reconnect controls and Select this template again where shown. Selecting the original template preserves existing mappings; load a different template from the main template loader only when you intend to start new mappings.

The document has blanks, wrong values, or leftover tags

Check the template spelling, field versus content brackets, and every mapping. For proposals, load the correct proposal again before mapping. For invoices, use exact supported invoice field names; that flow does not provide manual content mapping. Check nested fields inside reusable text too.

Generated documents are not a substitute for a final review. Correct the source data or template before generating another copy.

The document opened, but I cannot find its link on the record

For proposals, automatic linking requires starting with Load to Doc Builder on the proposal and waiting for the saved-URL confirmation. For invoices, use Generate & Save in the invoice itself.

If creation succeeded but saving the link failed, keep the new Google Docs URL immediately. Reopen the record to check before regenerating and accidentally making duplicate copies.

A save or invoice calculation fails

Read the error and keep the values you entered before leaving. Check the record after a failed save before retrying, so you do not create duplicates. Ask the owner for help if billing history cannot load or the totals do not match your records; do not send an invoice with unexplained totals.

Google shows a long website address instead of the studio name

The name shown on Google's screens is controlled by the app owner's Google branding setup. You cannot fix it by changing your own profile name. Ask the owner to check branding verification and publication.

Prepare for user acceptance testing

Use the separate Studio PM UAT Checklist provided with this guide. It contains steps, expected outcomes and blank result/notes tables. Its scenarios are not already marked passed.

Use an approved practice firm, fictional records with a distinctive UAT prefix, and consenting test accounts. A newly created firm is not a separate test environment: it still uses the app's actual data storage and any connected Google account. Create copies of templates, never use live client financial records as disposable test data, and do not send practice documents to clients.

Test the Owner and Member paths separately, including a Member who owns a different firm. Confirm both what is visible and what must be denied. Complete the Google cases with real authorized test accounts and check the source template as well as the generated copy. Report the exact screen, role, selected firm, steps, expected outcome and actual outcome; redact sensitive content and never include passwords, tokens or credential-bearing request URLs.

This documentation update does not claim that production was migrated, that the full app passed UAT, or that all live Google document workflows were verified.

Owner-only Google setup

Daily users should not need Google Cloud Console. The app operator manages Google branding, allowed website addresses, enabled APIs and selected-file permission configuration. Having the Owner role in a firm does not itself give you access to Google Cloud Console or the app's database.

The detailed owner checklist is in docs/google-oauth-setup.md in the project files. It covers the Google Picker API key and project number, branding verification/publication, and a live verification checklist. It is separate from this everyday user guide.

Before handing the app to users: The owner should verify real sign-in, selected-file permission, template loading, proposal/invoice/consultant document generation, and cancellation/reconnection with a fresh Google grant. No Google Cloud settings were changed as part of preparing this guide.
